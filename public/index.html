🔒 Hoknex Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

At Hoknex, we prioritize the privacy and security of students across global campuses. Here is how we handle your data:

1. Information Collection: We collect basic profile details (Name, Username, Department, and School Email) necessary to authenticate your student account and connect you with peers.

2. Media Storage: Uploaded profile pictures and banner images are stored securely on our servers solely for personalizing your campus profile.

3. Data Protection: We never sell student data to third parties. All passwords are encrypted securely using industry-standard hashing.

📜 Hoknex Terms of Service

Welcome to Hoknex! By registering and using our platform, you agree to the following guidelines:

1. Student Community Standards: Hoknex is a collaborative platform for schools and colleges worldwide. Respect your fellow peers across all interactions, posts, and comments.

2. Authentic Accounts: Users must register with valid student credentials or campus identifiers. Impersonation or malicious activity is strictly prohibited.

3. Platform Availability: We strive to keep Hoknex online 24/7, connected globally to support student innovation, hackathons, and campus networking.
